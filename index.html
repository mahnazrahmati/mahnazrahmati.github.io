01 — About Me
Where imagination meets precision, a piece begins to take shape.

I am Mahnaz Rahmati, a Jewelry Designer & Creative drawn to the meeting point of beauty, imagination and thoughtful design.

I enjoy exploring form, proportion, color and detail — especially the way gemstones can influence the mood and identity of a piece. For me, every concept begins with curiosity and develops through visual exploration.

Alongside jewelry design, I have a professional background in accounting and I am currently developing my knowledge of business development. This combination brings together creative thinking, precision and a practical understanding of business.

02 — Design Philosophy

Creating with Feeling

Jewelry, to me, is more than an object. It is a dialogue between form, emotion, color and story. Gemstones are often my starting point — their natural character, reflections and color can open the door to an entirely new idea.

Create. Explore. Transform.
